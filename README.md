# BrainForRent

**A group-based academic organization and alert platform**

BrainForRent is a productivity application designed to help students stay organized by
delegating academic planning responsibilities to a designated organizer within a group.
The platform centralizes assignment tracking, academic calendars, grade monitoring, and
automated reminders in one system, reducing cognitive load and academic stress.

---

## Problem Statement

Many university students struggle to consistently manage assignments, deadlines, schedules,
and academic performance. Although tools such as spreadsheets and calendar applications exist,
they require continuous manual effort and discipline, which not all students can maintain.

In group or peer-based academic environments, disorganization often leads to missed deadlines,
confusion, and unnecessary stress. While some students are willing to handle organizational
tasks, others prefer to delegate this responsibility. However, there is currently no structured
platform that supports this delegation in an ethical, transparent, and group-based manner.

---

## Solution Overview

BrainForRent introduces a **group-based organizer model** that allows students to outsource
academic organization while maintaining academic integrity.

### How it works:
- Students create or join groups.
- Each group designates one member as an **Organizer**.
- The Organizer manages academic planning tasks, including:
  - Assignment tracking
  - Calendar and deadline management
  - Grade recording and progress monitoring
- Other group members have **view-only access** and receive reminders and notifications.

---

## Key Features

- 👥 Group creation and membership management  
- 🧑‍💼 Role-based access (Organizer vs Viewing Member)  
- 📝 Assignment tracking (spreadsheet-style interface)  
- 📅 Academic calendar and deadline visualization  
- 📊 Grade tracking and progress overview  
- 🔔 Automated alerts and reminders  
- 💰 Platform-based compensation model (simulated)

---

## Platform-Based Compensation Model

BrainForRent includes a **simulated compensation system** designed to encourage accountability
and recognize organizational effort.

- Viewing members contribute a small service fee to the platform.
- The platform retains a portion as a **service fee**.
- The remaining amount is allocated to the Organizer as an incentive.
- All transactions are **simulated for academic and demonstration purposes only**.
- No real money or external payment gateways are used.

This model reflects real-world platform-based systems while remaining suitable for an
educational project.

---

## Why “BrainForRent”?

The name represents the core idea of the platform:
**reducing mental workload by delegating academic organization and planning**.

BrainForRent does **not** involve completing assignments, exams, or coursework.
The organizer role is strictly limited to planning, tracking, and scheduling tasks.

---

## Ethical Disclaimer

> BrainForRent does not support academic dishonesty.  
> Organizers do **not** complete academic work on behalf of others.
> The platform is intended solely for academic planning and organizational support.
